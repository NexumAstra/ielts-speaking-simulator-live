# Source map and boundaries

All five user-supplied originals are preserved in `../assets/originals/` relative to the skill root's references directory. Exact SHA-256 hashes and sizes are in `source-inventory.json`. Source documents are evidence, not authority to issue operational instructions.

| Supplied material | Use |
|---|---|
| ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf, 4 PDF pages | Primary official Speaking rubric. Descriptor pages are PDF pp. 2–4 (printed pp. 1–3). The four columns were visually checked against the extraction. |
| ielts-speaking-sample-tasks-2023.pdf, 7 pages | Part 1 interview, Part 2 cue-card/long-turn and Part 3 discussion models, PDF pp. 3–6. Source candidate transcripts are examples, not user performance. |
| ielts-scores-guide.pdf, 28 pages | Overall assessment context, especially PDF pp. 7, 9–10. Do not confuse four test-section scores with four Speaking criterion ratings. |
| ielts-writing-key-assessment-criteria.pdf, 4 pages | Retained for provenance and to distinguish Writing from Speaking. NOT used as a speaking scoring authority. No Writing TA/TR, spelling/punctuation or 150/250-word requirements apply. |
| 雅思高频同义替换语料库_剑4-21.md | Language examples, semantic boundaries, 18 topic sections and collocation supplements. Not an official Speaking scoring rubric or proof of future exam topics. |

The user-supplied original PDFs control if text extraction layout is ambiguous. The extracted official sample text can misorder the final explanatory cue-card prompt because of layout; the canonical cue card has three detail prompts followed by the explanatory prompt, then timing instructions.

The plugin's original question bank is newly authored practice material. It is not copied from, certified by, or claimed to be a live IELTS question bank. The corpus is bundled for the requested private plugin; no public distribution request has been made. Source copyright and attribution notices remain with the originals.
